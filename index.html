Oldfangled Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Oldfangled is a history channel on YouTube (@oldfangledofficial) and a Facebook Page (facebook.com/oldfangled). This policy covers the small private tools its owner uses to publish Oldfangled's own videos: the Facebook app "oldfangled-reels" and the Google app "channel-uploader".

What these tools do

They upload videos made by Oldfangled to Oldfangled's own YouTube channel and Facebook Page, set their titles, descriptions and thumbnails, and read basic statistics for those videos. They are used only by the channel's owner. They do not offer a login to anyone else and do not act on any other person's account, Page or channel.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store or share personal information about viewers or any other users. The tools receive only what is needed to manage Oldfangled's own channel and Page: access tokens for those accounts, video IDs, and view, like and share counts for Oldfangled's own videos. Statistics from YouTube Analytics are aggregated and contain no information about individual viewers.

Where it is kept

Access tokens and statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Data deletion

Because we hold no data about other people, there is nothing about you to delete. If you believe we hold any data about you, or you would like it removed, email arslanlmkt@gmail.com and we will respond and delete it within 30 days. The owner can revoke the tools' access at any time in Facebook and Google account settings, which also stops them working.

Google API Services

Oldfangled's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes

If these tools change what they access, this page will be updated and its date changed.
